<H2>A short list of books I valued</H2>

I recently got inspired by the website of Sarah Zerbes (my Linear Algebra professor at ETH back then) to add a list of books I’ve enjoyed reading.
Since I truly believe that books are unbelievably important — also for our personal lives — and that we should all read more 
(I know, it’s hard sometimes; I still catch myself skipping my reading time because of a lack of time... currently working on building better “book routine hygiene” ;)), 
here it is (chronological oder):

- **Children’s books**,  *by Lucy and Stephen Hawking ;)*   
- **Einsteins Schleier**, *by Anton Zeilinger*
- **Never Split the Difference**, *by Chris Voss*
- **Happier**, *by Tal Ben-Shahar*
- **Zu dumm für die Demokratie?**, *by Mark Schieritz*
- **Warum es die Welt nicht gibt**, *by Markus Gabriel*
- **Im Grunde gut**, *by Rutger Bregman*
                

I want to read:
